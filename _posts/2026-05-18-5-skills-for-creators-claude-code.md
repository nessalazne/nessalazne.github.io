## 1. Claude Video Vision

**[Claude Video Vision](https://github.com/jordanrendric/claude-video-vision)** is a Claude Code skill that lets you feed video files directly to Claude for analysis. Instead of manually watching through footage and writing your own notes, Claude watches for you — pulling out key moments, themes, and talking points.

For creators, this is huge. You can drop in a raw interview, a webinar recording, or even your own B-roll and ask Claude to extract the content brief automatically. That's hours of pre-production time gone.

**How to use it:**
1. Install the skill into your Claude Code setup
2. Pass any video file path as the input
3. Ask Claude to extract key points, write a summary, or identify the best clip moments

---

## 2. HyperFrames

**[HyperFrames](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes)** bridges the gap between Claude's visual design output and actual video. Once Claude generates a design or layout, HyperFrames turns it into an animated MP4 — no After Effects, no manual editing.

This one pairs perfectly with Claude's built-in design capabilities. Build your creative in Claude, export with HyperFrames, and you've got a polished short-form video ready for Reels or TikTok in minutes.

**Why it matters for creators:**
- No video editor skills required
- Works directly inside your Claude Code environment
- Produces clean, professional animated content at scale

## 3. Last 30 Days Skill

**[Last 30 Days Skill](https://github.com/mvanhorn/last30days-skill)** is a Claude Code skill that automatically reviews your recent content activity — posts, performance, topics — and surfaces patterns you'd otherwise miss.

Think of it as your AI content strategist that never forgets what you posted. Run it weekly and Claude gives you a clear picture of what's working, what's not, and what topics are trending in your feed.

**Use it to:**
- Audit your last month of content in seconds
- Identify your highest-performing topics
- Get Claude to suggest your next 5 post ideas based on real data

---

## 4. Supadata

[Supadata](https://supadata.ai/?ref=ness) is a research tool that pulls structured data from YouTube and other platforms so you can feed it directly into Claude for analysis. Instead of copying video transcripts by hand or scraping comments manually, Supadata does the heavy lifting and gives you clean, AI-ready output.

Creators use it for competitor research, keyword mining, and understanding why certain videos blow up. Pair it with a Claude prompt and you've got a full research system running in minutes.

**Best use cases:**
- Pull transcripts from top-performing videos in your niche
- Analyze comment sentiment at scale
- Feed YouTube data directly into your Claude research workflow

---

## 5. Kie AI

[Kie AI](https://kie.ai/) handles the media side of your AI workflow — uploading, hosting, and processing video and image files that need to move between tools. When you're building with Claude Code and need to pass media files to external APIs (like Blotato or HeyGen), Kie AI gives you clean, temporary public URLs so nothing breaks mid-workflow.

It's one of those tools you don't think about until you need it — and then you use it in every single project.

**Why creators need it:**
- Instantly host local video files for API use
- No cloud storage setup needed
- Works seamlessly inside Claude Code automations
