## What does the Content Engine actually do?

Content Engine turns one source into one posted short video, and the steps run in a fixed order. This is the sequence from the video:

1. You pick a source: a video, a reel, an article, or an idea you type yourself.
2. Claude writes the script in your voice and keeps the hook that already worked in the source.
3. The extension builds the video with your cloned voice, your avatar or no face at all, animated graphics drawn to match every line, and captions.
4. It writes a caption for every platform.
5. It posts the video, or schedules it.
6. When someone comments your keyword, it sends them your freebie by DM.

The idea is that you open Chrome in the morning and today's video is already made, approved and scheduled.

## How much setup does it need?

You set it up once, and the main job is connecting your accounts one time. The extension supports TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, Facebook, LinkedIn and more, nine platforms in total. After that, every video starts from the same place: open a source you like, or type an idea, and click once.

Before your first run, have these ready:

- Your social accounts, logged in and ready to connect
- A cloned voice, if you want your own voice on the video
- An avatar, or the decision to go faceless
- The word you want people to comment, which becomes your keyword

## What do I approve, and what does it cost?

You approve twice: the script first, then the video. Nothing that costs money runs until you say yes. That order matters because the script is the cheapest thing to fix. If the wording is wrong, you change it before any voice or graphics are generated.

A practical way to review the script:

- Check that the first line still matches the hook of the source
- Read it out loud once and cut anything you would not say
- Confirm the closing line asks for your keyword
