## What Are Claude Meetings, Whiteboards, Sheets and Data?

They are four separate tools inside Claude, and each one lines up with a kind of app you may already pay for. Here is the whole set as of November 2026.

| Claude tool | Stands in for | What it does | Limits to know |
|---|---|---|---|
| Claude Meetings | Granola, Otter | Joins your Google Meet call, takes notes, answers when you say its name, builds documents while you talk | Beta, Team and Enterprise plans only |
| Claude Whiteboards | Miro, FigJam | Turns a description into a laid-out board, such as a launch timeline with owners and dates | Beta on every plan, PNG export only |
| Claude Sheets | Google Sheets | Real formulas, charts and conditional formatting | 200,000 filled cells, no pivot tables yet |
| Claude Data | Power BI | Turns a connected source into a dashboard, a data story, a narrated video or a written summary | Needs a connected source such as BigQuery or Snowflake |

## What Do Claude Meetings and Claude Whiteboards Replace?

Claude Meetings covers the note-taker job and Claude Whiteboards covers the planning-board job. Meetings joins your Google Meet call as a participant. It takes notes, answers questions when you say its name, and builds documents while you are still talking. That is the same ground Granola or Otter cover.

The catch is the plan. Claude Meetings is in beta and only on Team and Enterprise plans, so a solo creator on a personal plan cannot swap out their note-taker with it yet.

Claude Whiteboards is easier to try because it is in beta on every plan. You describe your product launch and Claude lays it out as a timeline with owners and dates. It stands in for Miro or FigJam. The one limit to plan around is that you can export only as a PNG. If your team needs to keep editing the board in another tool, a flat image will not do that.

## Is Claude Sheets a Real Google Sheets Replacement?

Claude Sheets is a working spreadsheet with real formulas, charts and conditional formatting, and it has two limits. A sheet holds up to 200,000 filled cells, and there are no pivot tables yet. If your reporting lives in pivot tables, keep that file in Google Sheets for now.

The part I like most is scheduling. Ask Claude to schedule a weekly task that updates the sheet from your connected Stripe account. Your revenue sheet then fills itself in each week, and you stop copying numbers across by hand. ## What Does Claude Data Do Instead of Power BI?

Claude Data is Anthropic's answer to Power BI: you connect a data source and it builds the report for you. Connect a source like BigQuery or Snowflake and you can get four kinds of output:

1. A dashboard
2. A scrolling data story
3. A short narrated video
4. A printable written summary

That list matters because the same numbers often need to go to different people. A dashboard suits the person who checks numbers daily. A printable summary suits the person who wants one page.

## How Do You Check Which Subscriptions These Could Replace?

Check one subscription at a time, starting with the one that renews first. This is the order I use:

1. List every app you pay for that does meeting notes, whiteboards, spreadsheets or dashboards, with its renewal date.
2. Write down the two or three features you use in each one every week.
3. Match each app to the Claude tool in the table above and read the limits column.
4. Run one real piece of work through the Claude tool before the renewal date.
5. Cancel only the apps where nothing you use weekly is missing.

## FAQ

### Which Claude plans include Claude Meetings?

As of November 2026, Claude Meetings is in beta and available on Team and Enterprise plans only.

### Can I export a Claude Whiteboard as anything other than an image?

No. As of November 2026, Claude Whiteboards exports only as a PNG.

### Does Claude Sheets have pivot tables?

Not yet. Claude Sheets has real formulas, charts and conditional formatting, with a limit of 200,000 filled cells and no pivot tables as of November 2026.

### What data sources does Claude Data connect to?

Claude Data connects to a source like BigQuery or Snowflake. From there it can produce a dashboard, a scrolling data story, a short narrated video or a printable written summary.

### Should I cancel Otter, Miro, Google Sheets or Power BI right now?

Not before you test. Most of these Claude tools are in beta with limits, so run your real work through each one before your next renewal and cancel only what you no longer need.
