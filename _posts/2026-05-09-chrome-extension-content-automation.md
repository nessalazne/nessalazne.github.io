## What the Chrome Extension Actually Does

The extension sits in your browser and connects directly to your social media accounts. Once configured, you write or paste your content once — caption, media, scheduling time — and it queues it across every connected platform simultaneously.

**Key features:**
- **One input field** for your caption — the extension adapts the format per platform
- **Multi-platform posting** — Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn, and more
- **Built-in scheduling** — set a time once, it publishes everywhere at the right moment
- **No re-uploading** — link your media once, the extension handles the rest

The 3-hour → 2-minute comparison in the video isn't an exaggeration. The bottleneck was always the repetition, not the content itself.

## How to Set It Up in Under 20 Minutes

**Step 1 — Install the extension**

Add it to Chrome from the Chrome Web Store. Once installed, you'll see the extension icon in your browser toolbar. Click it to open the dashboard.

**Step 2 — Connect your accounts**

Go to Settings → Accounts and authenticate each platform. The extension uses OAuth — you're logging in through the platform's own auth flow, not handing over passwords. Connect Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, and any others you use.

**Step 3 — Create your first post**

Click New Post. Add your caption, upload or link your media, and pick your scheduling time. At the bottom you'll see toggles for each connected platform — turn on the ones you want to hit.

**Step 4 — Hit Schedule**

That's it. The extension takes over from here. It formats the caption for each platform, applies the right hashtag strategy, and publishes at the time you set. You get a confirmation when each one goes live.
